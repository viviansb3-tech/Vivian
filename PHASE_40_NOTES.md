# Phase 40 — Research Intelligence

Added an interactive Research Ecosystem section to the portfolio.

## Features
- 13 research/project nodes covering multivariate statistics, time series, spatial statistics, simulation and software.
- Filter controls for discipline.
- Clickable nodes with contextual research detail.
- Research timeline linking foundation, expansion, application and honours research.
- Mobile-responsive glassmorphism layout.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Preserves existing project links and previous phases.

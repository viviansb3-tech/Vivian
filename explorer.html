THE EFFECT OF AUTOCORRELATION AND CROSS-CORRELATION ON HOTELLING'S T² CONTROL CHARTS

A simulation-based investigation of how dependence structures affect the performance of the traditional Hotelling's T² control chart.

SIMULATION SCENARIOS

Four controlled scenarios distinguish the individual and joint effects of dependence.

01

INDEPENDENT

Autocorrelation absent. Cross-correlation absent. The classical benchmark.

02

CROSS-CORRELATED

Dependence between variables is introduced through the covariance structure.

03

AUTOCORRELATED

Serial dependence is controlled through the autoregressive parameter.

04

AUTO + CROSS-CORRELATED

Both dependence structures are present, providing the combined case.

RESEARCH PIPELINE
01SIMULATEGenerate correlated multivariate process data in R.
02CONTROL DEPENDENCEVary autoregressive and cross-correlation parameters.
03MONITORApply the traditional Hotelling's T² control chart.
04EVALUATECompare chart behaviour using ARL and SDRL.

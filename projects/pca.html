23 variables

The PCA used 23 Likert-scale questionnaire items from 2,571 complete respondents.

4 retained PCs

Parallel analysis and the Kaiser criterion retained four principal components.

50.3% cumulative variance

PC1–PC4 jointly explained 50.3% of total variance; PC1 alone explained 31.7%.

SS loadings

The retained-component SS loadings were 7.290, 1.739, 1.317 and 1.227.

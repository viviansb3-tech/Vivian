MULTIVARIATE STATISTICS / SIMULATION

Hotelling's T² Control-Chart Simulation

A simulation framework developed to study Average Run Length behaviour of Hotelling's T² under different dependence structures and mean-shift conditions.

Research focus

Evaluate how independence, autocorrelation and cross-correlation alter multivariate control-chart performance.

Simulation design

Vary sample size, autocorrelation strength, cross-correlation and mean shift; generate multivariate processes repeatedly and record run lengths.

Scenarios

Independent; autocorrelated; cross-correlated; combined autocorrelation + cross-correlation.

Performance measures

ARL, SDRL, control-limit behaviour and sensitivity to shifts.

Portfolio value

Provides a bridge from theoretical multivariate statistics to reproducible Monte Carlo simulation and statistical process monitoring.

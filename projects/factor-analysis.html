SELECTED WORK / FACTOR ANALYSIS

Big Five Factor Analysis

A confirmatory analysis of the proposed five-factor structure of personality using 25 items and responses from 2,800 individuals.

01

RESEARCH QUESTION

Test whether correlations among the 25 items are consistent with the pre-specified five-factor structure.

02

DATA

The dataset contains responses from 2,800 individuals to 25 short statements, rated on a six-point scale.

03

FACTOR EXTRACTION

Determine the number of factors using data-driven criteria together with the theory-driven five-factor structure.

04

ROTATION & LOADINGS

Rotate the solution and check whether each item's largest loading falls on its theoretically assigned factor.

05

CONSTRUCT VALIDITY

Close correspondence would support the construct validity of the 25-item instrument; systematic departures would call it into question.

Spatial Distribution of Bloemfontein Schools

A spatial-statistics investigation of school locations in Bloemfontein, combining GIS visualisation with point-pattern and spatial-autocorrelation methods.

Research focus

Determine whether schools are randomly distributed, clustered or dispersed and identify areas of spatial concentration and accessibility.

Methods

Quadrat Count Analysis; Average Nearest Neighbour; Nearest Neighbour Ratio; Ripley's K; Kernel Density Estimation; Voronoi diagrams; Global Moran's I.

Findings represented in the project

The analysis identified a strong central concentration of schools, with central suburbs such as Westdene, Willows, Arboretum, Brandwag and Universitas forming a prominent cluster. Walking-buffer analysis highlighted stronger accessibility around the centre than peripheral areas.

Technical scope

CSV data preparation, longitude/latitude handling, GIS mapping, spatial summaries and interpretation of point patterns.

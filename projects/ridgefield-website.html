Ridgefield Website Reconstruction

A source-backed reconstruction project for an education website, organising Cambridge, CAPS and primary-school information into a clearer responsive web experience.

Rebuild and restructure a school website so curriculum, campus and tuition information can be discovered more clearly across desktop and mobile screens.

Content architecture

The project works across Ridgefield's Cambridge, CAPS and Primary offerings, including subject information, tuition messaging and location-specific school content.

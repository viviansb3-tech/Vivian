Canonical Correlation Analysis

An analysis of the relationship between fear of computers and fear of statistics using canonical correlation analysis.

01

RESEARCH QUESTION

Examine whether, and how, a set of computer-fear items relates to a set of statistics-fear items.

02

DATA

The study uses seven computer-fear items and eight statistics-fear items from 2,571 respondents on a five-point Likert scale.

03

CANONICAL FUNCTIONS

Identify underlying dimensions that summarise shared variation between the two anxiety domains.

04

RELIABILITY

Determine which canonical functions are statistically reliable.

05

INTERPRETATION

Interpret reliable dimensions in terms of the original items and the two anxiety domains.
